# FlowBoost Product Context

## Product
FlowBoost sells targeted website traffic through campaign-based delivery. The product does not sell SMM services, social engagement packages, followers, likes, or reseller services.

## Primary audience
Independent operators, growth teams, creators, and small businesses who need to launch and monitor a website-traffic campaign without a media-buying dashboard's complexity.

## Core job
A user chooses a destination URL, traffic source, visitor volume, and delivery speed; funds their wallet; launches the campaign; and monitors delivery and spend.

## Non-negotiable functions
Authentication, Firestore-backed user profile and balance, campaign creation, campaign list/detail states, wallet top-ups through Flutterwave, transaction history, settings, and automatic campaign status/delivery updates.

## Product language
Use traffic, visitors, destinations, sources, delivery, reach, spend, wallet, campaign, and delivery status. Do not introduce SMM, reseller, service catalog, engagement, followers, likes, or package language.

## Trust constraints
Avoid invented performance claims. Make cost, delivery timing, balance impact, and paused/complete states explicit. Preserve accessible controls, responsive behavior, and clear error states.
